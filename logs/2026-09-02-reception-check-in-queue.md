# Reception Check-in Queue

## Scope

The Receptionist check-in queue provides a focused front-desk view for accepted
and checked-in appointments. It is separate from booking and schedule management
and displays administrative patient and appointment information only.

## Planned workflow

1. Open the Check-in Queue, defaulted to the current Singapore date.
2. Filter by date, Doctor, patient ID/name, or queue status.
3. Select an appointment to view permitted administrative details.
4. Check in an accepted appointment once its scheduled start time has arrived.
5. The queue refreshes automatically and the appointment moves to checked-in.

No real patient data is recorded in this log.
